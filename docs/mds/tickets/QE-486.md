# QE-486 — Reconcile the regime volatility split with its consumer: document it as descriptive full-sample, or make it causal  *(R5 — panel review 2026-07-31, P2)*

> **Status:** Todo — [panel review](../../reviews/panel-review-2026-07-31.md) · [backlog index](../../backlog.md) · [spec](../../specs.md)

`Phase: Review R5 (panel review)` · `Area: signal (regime)` · `Depends on / relates to: relates to QE-480 (regime hash determinism), QE-127 (DE objective consumer)`

**Why.** The volatility regime split classifies **every** bar against a **full-sample, future-informed
median**: the median is computed over *all* defined rolling vols `sorted = vols.filter_map(..)` across
the whole series (`crates/signal/src/regime.rs:138-148`), then each bar is labelled `if v > median`
(`regime.rs:153`). (Path correction: the labeller is in **`qe-signal`**, not `qe-ensemble` — the
review's `crates/ensemble/src/regime.rs:139` citation points at the QE-127 *consumer* crate; the split
itself is `crates/signal/src/regime.rs:153`.) This is a genuine **look-ahead leak**: a bar early in the
series is labelled Volatile/Calm using volatilities that only materialise later.

Verification confirmed the leak is **latent, not live**: the current consumer is `label_regimes` →
`holdout_regime_composition` in the train job (`crates/cli/src/jobs/train.rs:171,175,285`), a
**descriptive holdout composition tally** — a full-sample median over a frozen holdout is benign for a
descriptive count. But the module docstring advertises the labeller as feeding "the DE objective (QE-127,
in `qe-ensemble`)" (`regime.rs:6`), and the QE-127 regime-aware CV (`crates/ensemble/src/regime.rs`)
selects per-fold. **If** the full-sample-median labels are ever wired into per-fold DE selection as that
docstring implies, the future-informed split becomes a live look-ahead leak into the objective — the
invariant is **no bar may use future data to influence selection.**

**Scope / requirements.**
- **Reconcile intent and code — pick one:**
  - **Descriptive posture:** document `label_regimes` (and the median split at `regime.rs:138-153`) as
    **full-sample, descriptive-only**, and assert it must **not** feed per-fold selection. Fix the
    misleading QE-127/"DE objective" line in the module docstring (`regime.rs:6`) so it no longer implies
    selection use, or route selection through a separate causal labeller.
  - **Causal posture:** if the split *is* to feed the objective, make it **causal** — an
    expanding/rolling median that uses only bars up to `i` — so no bar is classified using future vols.
- Either way, the QE-127 docstring must match the actual consumer; the current text is the defect as much
  as the code.

**Out of scope.** The `ln()`/hash-determinism concern (QE-480). The trend/efficiency-ratio axis. Any
change to the **current descriptive tally's output** unless the split is deliberately made causal (which
would change composition counts and is a conscious behaviour change, not a silent one).

**Acceptance criteria.**
- The module docstring (`crates/signal/src/regime.rs:6`, and the `label_regimes` doc at `regime.rs:95-99`)
  accurately states the consumer and the full-sample-vs-causal nature of the median split.
- **If descriptive:** a test/assertion documents that `label_regimes` is not consumed by per-fold DE
  selection (the consumer today is `holdout_regime_composition`, train-only); no output change.
- **If made causal:** a test proves the volatility median at bar `i` uses **only** bars `≤ i` (no future
  data), and the composition-count change is intentional and golden-updated.
